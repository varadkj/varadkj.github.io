## What I do

My main specialization is network and host penetration testing. The CPTS lane: enumeration, exploitation, privesc, lateral movement, and writing up the chain at the end. I like breaking things and pushing systems to their limits, finding the failure modes their builders didn't see coming. The capstone took that work into OT/IoT territory, and that's becoming a real second specialty.

Beyond the technical side, I think security is a mindset before it's a skillset. The same instinct that makes someone glance over their shoulder for a CCTV camera before unlocking their phone in public is the same instinct that catches a misconfigured Kerberos delegation in an Active Directory pentest. It's a way of seeing systems, including the social and physical ones, as composed of trust boundaries that someone, somewhere, is going to test. Once you can't turn that off, you don't.

I'm also an audiophile, and that one needs a sentence of defense because the word gets misused. Music is probably the most universal form of art humans have made. Every civilisation we have records of made music before they made almost anything else, and I think that fact deserves more respect than it usually gets. We live in a digital age where the chain between artist and listener runs through a lot of hardware and software, and most of it quietly degrades what the artist actually committed to tape. Being an audiophile is just caring enough about that chain to hear what they intended. Master the bridge between artist and listener, and the rest follows.
